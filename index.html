Stoicism

A quiet philosophy for loud times. Stoicism is not about escaping pain, but about meeting reality with courage, clarity, and self-command. This is a personal sanctuary by Ritish Lamsal.

The Core Idea

Stoicism begins with a simple distinction: some things are within our control, and some things are not. Suffering arises when we confuse the two. Freedom comes from aligning our effort only with what depends on us.

"Make the best use of what is in your power, and take the rest as it happens." — Epictetus

Control

Your judgments, intentions, and actions are yours alone. Guard them carefully.

Discipline

Comfort weakens. Discipline strengthens. Choose the harder right over the easier wrong.

Virtue

Wisdom, courage, justice, and temperance are the only true measures of success.

The Stoic Day

Morning
Mentally rehearse difficulty. Decide to act with virtue.
Midday
Do one thing at a time. Give full attention to duty.
Evening
Review the day without excuse or self-hatred.

Practices That Shape Character

  • Voluntary discomfort to reduce fear of hardship
  • Negative visualization to cultivate gratitude
  • Journaling to correct distorted thinking
  • Silence to regain mental clarity
  • Service to others as moral training

Why This Site Exists

This site is not meant to motivate, sell, or perform. It exists as a reminder: life is short, control is limited, and character is everything.

"Waste no more time arguing what a good man should be. Be one." — Marcus Aurelius

स्टोइक दर्शन

कोलाहलपूर्ण समयका लागि शान्त दर्शन। स्टोइक दर्शन दुःखबाट भाग्ने उपाय होइन, तर जीवनलाई साहस, स्पष्टता र आत्मसंयमका साथ सामना गर्ने कला हो। रितिश लाम्साल को व्यक्तिगत प्रयास।

मूल विचार

स्टोइक दर्शन एउटा सरल विभाजनबाट सुरु हुन्छ — केही कुरा हाम्रो नियन्त्रणमा छन्, केही छैनन्। यी दुईलाई गडबड गर्दा नै दुःख उत्पन्न हुन्छ।

"जे तिम्रो हातमा छ त्यसको सर्वोत्तम प्रयोग गर, बाँकीलाई स्वाभाविक रूपमा स्वीकार गर।" — एपिक्टेटस

नियन्त्रण

तिम्रो सोच, निर्णय र कर्म तिम्रै हुन्। तिनको रक्षा गर।

अनुशासन

सजिलो बाटोले कमजोर बनाउँछ। अनुशासनले बलियो बनाउँछ।

सद्गुण

ज्ञान, साहस, न्याय र संयम नै सफल जीवनका साँचो मापदण्ड हुन्।

  • दैनिक लेखनद्वारा सोच सुधार्ने

  • अरूको सेवा गरेर नैतिक बल विकास

यस साइटको उद्देश्य
